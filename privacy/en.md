# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what data the “114” app (the Quran with word-by-word analysis) processes, why, and how you can control it.

## Without an account

The app works without signing in. Bookmarks, saved words, your reading position and progress, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

## Sign-in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and account ID through Firebase Authentication (Google). We use them to show your profile and to sync your data between devices.

## Sync

After you sign in, the following is sent to and stored on our server (Cloudflare): bookmarks, saved words, reading position and progress, and your AI assistant chats and question history. This keeps your data safe and available on all your devices. When you sign out, your data is first saved to the server and then removed from the phone.

## AI assistant (Premium)

Your question, the earlier messages of that chat, the text of the verse or word you are asking about, and the app language are sent to our server and from there to an artificial intelligence service to get an answer. Please do not include personal information in your questions. The number of questions per day is counted for your subscription limit.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment details; we never see them. Our server receives purchase details from the app (the product ID and purchase token) to verify the subscription with Google and turn on Premium.

## Downloaded texts and audio

- Interface texts, word translations and this document are downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
- Verse audio is loaded from everyayah.com; surah audio and the list of reciters come from mp3quran.net.

These services see your IP address, as with any website you open. We do not share any other information about you with them.

## What we do not do

- We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
- We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.
- We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

## Retention and deletion

Data on our server is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account in your profile: your server data (sync data, Premium status, counters) and the account itself will be deleted. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and the date at the top will be updated.
